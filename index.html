DONATE SAFELY

Before you donate

  • Generally aged 18–65 years
  • Meet the blood bank's weight and haemoglobin requirements
  • Feeling well on the day of donation
  • Meet the required interval since last donation
  • Inform staff about medicines, illness or recent procedures
Medical disclaimer

This is general information only. Final eligibility is determined by a qualified blood bank or medical professional after screening. Never rely on blood group alone; hospitals perform cross-matching before transfusion.
